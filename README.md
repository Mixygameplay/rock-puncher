Rock Puncher

A 2D side-scrolling incremental game about punching a rock. Walk up to the rock, punch it, collect stones, and head back to the shop to upgrade your gear.

Play it here:
  https://mixygameplay.github.io/rock-puncher/

How to play
  Action	Control
  Walk left / right	A / D or the arrow keys
  Punch the rock	Left-click the rock (you must be standing next to it)
  Open the shop	Left-click the shop building (you must be standing next to it)
  Leave the shop	Click Leave shop or press Esc
  Every stone takes 5 damage to break off. Damage carries over, so if a punch deals more than you need, the extra counts toward the next stone. Collect stones, walk back to the shop, and buy upgrades.

Upgrades
  Upgrade	Cost	Effect
  Tough Fists	25 stones	Each punch deals 2 damage instead of 1
  
Current features
  Side-view stick figure with walking and punching animations
  Click-to-punch mining with damage that carries over between stones
  A clickable shop with a purchasable upgrade
  Progress saves automatically in your browser (stones, damage progress, and upgrades owned)
  Reset progress link to start over
  Visual feedback: rock shake, flying chips, and floating "+1 stone" text
  Automatic light and dark color schemes
